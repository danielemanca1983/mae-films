      About

      Finding the heart of it

      I’m Mae, a filmmaker and editor drawn to honest, human stories: documentary, short film and the in-between. I direct, shoot, write and edit, and I love taking hours of raw footage and finding the heart of it.

      My work moves from intimate personal films about the body, the land and care, to documentaries, event trailers and online content for other people’s projects.
